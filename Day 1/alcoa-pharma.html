📋 Pharmaceutical Data Integrity Framework

ALCOA+ (Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate, and plus) ensures data integrity in pharmaceutical operations. This assessment tool aligns with FDA 21 CFR Part 11 and ICH GxP guidelines for electronic and paper-based records. Compliance is mandatory for all GMP, GLP, and GDP operations.

Facility & Operations Information

ALCOA+ Principles

A

Attributable

Data traceable to the person who created/modified it with user IDs, timestamps, and audit trails

L

Legible

Data is clear, readable, and understandable; properly formatted with no ambiguity

C

Contemporaneous

Data recorded at the time an event occurs; no retroactive or backdated entries

O

Original

Data maintained in original form without erasure; corrections traceable and signed

A

Accurate

Data is correct, complete, and precise; verified and matches source documents

+

Plus

Additional considerations: security, system validation, disaster recovery, and regulatory expectations

Data Integrity Compliance Checklist

Attributable (A) - User Accountability & Traceability

Legible (L) - Clarity & Readability

Contemporaneous (C) - Timely Recording

Original (O) - Data Integrity & Preservation

Accurate (A) - Data Correctness & Completeness

Plus (+) - Additional Requirements & Security
