❓ Help & Support

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new medicine?

Go to Inventory → Add Medicine and fill in the required details including name, category, batch number, quantity, price, and expiry date.

What does Low Stock Alert mean?

When a medicine's quantity falls below the threshold (default: 20 units), it appears in the Low Stock Alert section. You can adjust this threshold in Settings.

How do I create an order?

Go to Orders → Create Order, select either Stock In or Stock Out, choose the medicine, enter quantity, and add reference details.

Can I track supplier information?

Yes! Go to Suppliers to add and manage supplier details. You can link suppliers to orders.

How do I generate reports?

Go to Reports to generate inventory reports, order reports, and analytics. You can export them as PDF.
